1. Nature of the Marks and Services

Applicant Travis Barrett LeBlanc is the sole creator and source of Totem Astrology, a personal interpretive system developed over the past several decades. Totem Astrology is distinct from traditional Chinese fortune-telling or generic BaZi divination. It is offered as a tool for self-understanding and relationship insight through four totem animal guides (Spirit, Mind, Heart, and Body), Nepal Time calculation, Values Triangles, renamed totem animals, educational website content, and personal reading services.

Applicant uses the marks in connection with services including, as selected on the application:

  • Personal astrology and totem-guide readings (video consultation)
  • Educational information about the Totem Astrology system
  • Online Totem Animals Calculator and related web tools at totemastrology.com
  • Relationship, family, and personal-growth guidance using the Totem Astrology framework

Suggested International Classes: Class 41 (education/entertainment; providing information in the field of astrology), Class 45 (personal consulting; astrology readings), and optionally Class 42 (online software/calculator tools), as applicable.

4. How the Mark Has Acquired Distinctiveness

4.1 Inherent / suggestive distinctiveness

The mark TOTEM ASTROLOGY is used exclusively by applicant Travis Barrett LeBlanc to identify a specific personal system of astrology interpretation—not generic astrology or traditional Chinese Four Pillars fortune-telling. The combined term refers to applicant’s original methodology (four totem animal guides, Nepal Time calculation, renamed totem animals, Values Triangles, Rainbow and Shadow companions, and related educational content) offered at totemastrology.com and in personal reading services.

The mark does not describe a generic category of service; it identifies applicant as the single source of this named system. Applicant has used the mark consistently in connection with these services and presents it as his own created and personally refined system.

4.2 Acquired distinctiveness / secondary meaning (Section 2(f), if required)

Through continuous use in commerce, the mark TOTEM ASTROLOGY has acquired distinctiveness as a source indicator for applicant’s services.

Consumers encountering TOTEM ASTROLOGY in connection with four-totem-guide readings, the totemastrology.com website, and applicant’s described methodology understand the mark to refer to applicant and applicant’s services—not to astrology services in general.
