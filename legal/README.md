# Trademark evidence document

## File

`trademark-evidence.html` — consolidated USPTO support statement for both applications:

- **Word mark:** TOTEM ASTROLOGY (standard characters)
- **Design mark:** logo with tagline

Includes mark descriptions, color claim, specimen language, distinctiveness statement, and a signature block.

## Before you submit

1. Open `trademark-evidence.html` in Chrome or Safari.
2. Fill in every **[bracketed]** field (first use dates, filing basis, years of development, commercial services, etc.).
3. **Print → Save as PDF**.
4. Sign and date the PDF (print, sign, scan — or add a digital signature).
5. Upload the PDF to the USPTO as supplemental evidence / statement of distinctiveness, as the form allows.

## Also upload separately

- Logo JPEG: `assets/images/totem-astrology-logo-trademark.jpg`
- Website specimen screenshots (homepage showing mark in use), if filing Section 1(a)

## Not legal advice

Review with a trademark attorney before filing if possible.
