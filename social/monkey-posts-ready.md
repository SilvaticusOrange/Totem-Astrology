## 1) Facebook hub (long) + square image

Born to play the board beneath the board! That is the gleeful genius of the Monkey — ninth animal of the Totem Zodiac, Mage of Power & Prestige. Monkeys understand power dynamics the way lungs understand air: innate, mostly subconscious, occasionally mischievous, often three moves ahead while smiling like they’re only here for the bananas.

Still Monkey: clever, theatrical when useful, invisible when smarter. Games within games. Credit optional. Outcome mandatory. Their power is Man-Behind-the-Curtain energy — Wizard of Oz logic with better timing. What looks like intricate machination from outside is, to them, simply *obvious*. Sometimes they even play less informed on purpose, slowly walking everyone else around to the winning perspective without a public duel.

Monk and Monkey sound alike for a reason: both care more about what’s happening backstage than about who gets the standing ovation. As Mage of this triangle, they can appear to have no power at all. That’s the costume. The real work is offstage, where the levers are.

This Fire Horse year is a playground and a stress test. Brush fires create chaos — and chaos is where Monkey reads the board fastest. Don’t waste the gift on petty wins. Use the Mage mind for outcomes that actually matter: protect the treasure, free the stuck, outsmart the trap without needing your name in lights.

If you were born in a Monkey year, the long game loves you. Play it with heart, not only with tricks. Prestige without purpose is just a shiny cage. 🐒🎭
