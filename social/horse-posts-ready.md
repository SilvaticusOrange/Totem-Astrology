## 1) Facebook hub (long) + square image

Born to run toward the horizon! That is the Holy Hunger of the Horse — seventh animal of the Totem Zodiac, Seeker of Inspiration & Idealism. And yes: this is a **Fire Horse** year, so the whole sky is speaking Horse language — speed, breakthrough, brush fire, freedom that won’t sit still in a too-small stall.

Horses don’t admire ideals from a polite distance. They *gallop* toward them. Freedom is currency. Wind-in-the-mane is conviction made physical. When something captures their imagination, the whole being surges: senses wide, mundane world left in dust and promise. An ideal without motion is unbearable. So they run, leap, and lead from the front — and somehow half the herd finds itself moving too.

Passionate to a fault. Sometimes mistaking the thrill of the run for arrival. That restlessness is also the gift: infectious momentum that wakes whole groups to longing they didn’t know they still had. Horizon hunger. Brotherhood and sisterhood on the move. Dignity that refuses a cage.

In 2026 the pace belongs to this energy — for better and for wilder. Ride it; don’t let it ride you. Brush fires will try to scatter your attention into a dozen fake urgencies. Horse wisdom says: pick the true horizon, then commit your hooves. Inspiration Seeker doesn’t mean chaotic — it means devoted to what could be, with enough courage to leave what merely is.

If you were born in a Horse year — or you’re simply breathing this Fire Horse atmosphere — remember: speed without soul is just stampede. Run for the vision. Carry others in your wake. The open ridge is calling. 🐴🔥
