## 1) Facebook hub (long) + square image

Born to know what’s under the surface! That is the quiet Truth of the Snake — sixth animal of the Totem Zodiac, Mage of Stability & Security. Lying near the deep turn of the wheel, spending so much of life in full contact with the Earth, the Snake doesn’t need loud proclamations of safety. It *embodies* the mystery of what Security really means — and can find it nearly anywhere, including places other people walk past screaming.

Still Snake: transformation, timing, initiation, the wisdom that sheds skin instead of pretending last year’s armor still fits. Not flashy like a parade. Not absent either. Stability worked behind the curtain — felt in the bones, read in the undercurrent, chosen in the pause before the strike or the soft retreat that saves the whole operation.

Snakes sense fault lines before the quake. They know which “safe” pastures are theater and which foundations will hold. During this crazy Fire Horse year, when safe ground seems to catch flame from every direction, Snake silence is not emptiness — it’s strategy. While the prairie burns loud, the Mage of Security listens for the still point, sheds what’s dead, and keeps the living thread intact.

Bloodless panic helps no one. Neither does denial. Snake energy says: feel the truth of the ground under you, transform what must transform, and protect the real treasure — not the costume of security, the *substance* of it. Initiation isn’t comfortable. It’s clarifying.

If you were born in a Snake year, trust the knowing in your belly. The world needs your depth more than your performance. 🐍🖤
