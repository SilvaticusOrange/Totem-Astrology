The Bountiful Bear finds the pot of gold at the end of the rainbow, enjoys the honey, and shares it with the dearest — sweetness passed around without tally or condition. True harmony isn’t the absence of difficulty. It’s the presence of refuge: joy tasted, rest taken, life juicy again.

If you thought you were a Pig, surprise: in Totem Astrology your guide becomes **Bear** — same ancient blessing of abundance and ease, renamed so modern hearts can feel the protective warmth, the sensual Earthly Queen energy, and the sacred right to rest.

Unhurried authority. Peace and plenty as how life *ought* to feel.
