## 1) Facebook hub (long) + square image

**Mind** is the animal of your **birth month** — how you think, learn, plan, and process the world — and its **element** flavors that thinking.

If Spirit is the horizon of your life, Mind is the weather system inside your head: the style of your attention, the way you solve problems, the flavor of your curiosity, the mental habits you reach for under pressure.

Right now we’re in the **Month of the Wood Deer**. Someone with a Wood Deer Mind may think in soft openings and careful paths — sensing the room before committing, preferring growth that feels natural over force, noticing what needs gentle tending. Under a Fire Horse Spirit, that can look like: a lifelong drive to *run* toward freedom, processed through a mind that won’t trample the forest to get there. Both can be true. One doesn’t cancel the other.

Wood doesn’t make the Deer “softer” in a weak sense — it makes the intelligence *alive*: branching, growing, connecting.

Mind isn’t “smarter” or “dumber.” It’s *how* intelligence moves through you — quick dart, deep chew, bold leap, patient survey.

When you and a partner “don’t speak the same language,” it often starts here: two Mind animals trying to solve the same problem with different tools. Understanding the map doesn’t force agreement. It just makes the disagreement less personal.
