## 1) Facebook hub (long) + square image

**Heart** is the animal of your **birth day** — how you feel, relate, and choose in the intimate theater of daily life — sharpened or softened by its **element**.

Spirit may set the kingdom. Mind may map the strategy. Heart decides what happens in the room: attachment, tenderness, pride, retreat, loyalty, the yes and the no that aren’t always logical — and don’t need to be.

Today is a **Wood Dragon** day. Someone with a Wood Dragon Heart may love the way Dragons do — with standards, dignity, and a fierce sense of what (and who) is worth protecting — but Wood gives that heartroom to grow: patronage that expands, bonds that branch into new possibility, care that invests in what could become excellent. Their Spirit could be a Fire Horse and their Mind a Wood Deer — still, day-to-day emotional weather runs through Dragon-with-wood: excellence matters, loyalty matters, and mediocrity in close relationships doesn’t sit easy.

Heart is why two people with the same Spirit year can still love differently. Same crown; different pulse.

If you’ve ever thought, “I know what I *should* do — why can’t I feel my way there?” — look at Heart. That’s often where the real conversation lives.
