## 1) Facebook hub (long) + square image

You don’t have one animal — you have **four**.

In Totem Astrology, your birth year, month, day, and hour each reveal a totem animal guide — and each one carries an **element** too. Together they stack into one totem pole:

**Spirit** (Year) — crowned at the top  
**Mind** (Month)  
**Heart** (Day)  
**Body** (Hour) — grounded at the base

These aren’t four separate horoscopes taped together. They’re four facets of a single birth moment — four allies speaking through different layers of your life.

Right now, for example, the Earth shows:
**Fire Horse** as Spirit · **Wood Deer** as Mind · **Metal Buffalo** as Heart · **Earth Squirrel** as Body.

**Spirit** arcs over the whole story: the big decisions, the central beliefs about what matters, the lifelong “why.”  
**Mind** shapes how you think, learn, plan, and make sense of the world.  
**Heart** moves through feeling, relationship, and the intimate choices of daily life.  
**Body** carries instinct, stamina, and the physical rhythm you move through the world with.

A Fire Horse Spirit doesn’t mean you’re “a Horse person” and nothing else. It means Horse energy — lit by Fire — is guiding the broad arc, while your Mind, Heart, and Body may speak in completely different animal *and* elemental languages underneath.

That’s the gift of the totem pole: complexity with a map. Not fate. Not a cage. Four guides you can consult — Spirit to Body — whenever life gets loud.

Over the next four days we’ll walk each level: Spirit, then Mind, then Heart, then Body.
