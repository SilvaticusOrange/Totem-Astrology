During this wild Fire Horse year, ideals get stress-tested in public. Brush fires of distraction, outrage, and speed try to scatter everyone’s attention — but the Tiger was built for heat. Fire doesn’t scare them; lukewarm does. This year asks them to lead by example when the pace goes feral, to hold the center of Inspiration while others lose the plot, and to protect what they love without burning the whole forest down.

If you were born in a Tiger year, you already know: the world needs your flame, not your apology. Stand taller. Guard the vision. Let the room catch fire for the right reasons. 🐅🔥
