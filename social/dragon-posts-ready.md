## 1) Facebook hub (long) + square image

Born to reign! That is the Truth coiled at the center of the Dragon — fifth animal of the Totem Zodiac, Royal of Power & Prestige. Dragons don’t chase power like a desperate hobby. They *have* it, and they wield it according to a confident inner map of how things ought to be. Domain. Treasure. Kingdom. Call it what you want — if it’s under their purview, it belongs in their care, and they know quality when they see it.

Still Dragon, still unmistakable. Protectors of what they love with a possessive loyalty that can intimidate… and also keep the gold from getting stolen by mediocrity. They can be exacting, theatrical, even exhausting to those who prefer smallness — but that intensity is guardianship. Face matters. Standards matter. The center of the stage isn’t vanity; it’s where they naturally stand when the story needs a spine.

Even when they’re not “technically” in charge, Dragons find a way to issue directions anyway — often to the chagrin of whoever holds the official title. That’s not always polite. It *is* often correct. Prestige Royal energy commands the middle of the circle because someone has to decide what counts as treasure and what gets left in the ashes.

This Fire Horse year stress-tests kingdoms. Brush fires of haste and hype try to redefine value overnight. Dragons: refine the hoard. Defend what deserves defending. Let the rest burn off. Pressure doesn’t invent your authority — it reveals whether you’ve been using it for glory alone or for the people and projects that make a domain worth ruling.

If you were born in a Dragon year, the world already feels your weather system. Own it with wisdom. Treasure is a responsibility. 🐉👑
