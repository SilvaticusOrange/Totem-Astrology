## 1) Facebook hub (long) + square image

Born to keep the flame through the long night! That is the deep Truth of the Wolf — eleventh animal of the Totem Zodiac. Where Dog once stood for loyalty, Totem Astrology howls as **The Wolf**: pack devotion with a wild edge, Inspiration that survives when the applause stops, ideals that know what they cost.

Wolf is Mage of Inspiration & Idealism — strategic idealist, not naive cheerleader. They sense which visions will endure and which will burn out cute and useless. They invest accordingly: in people, in causes, in the long dark before dawn. Songs in darkness. Plans in silence. Loyalty that doesn’t need a parade. Inspiration here is relational and wild — rooted in belonging, unafraid of the untamed edge.

If you thought you were a Dog, surprise: in Totem Astrology your guide becomes **Wolf** — same fierce faithfulness, renamed so modern hearts can feel the pack, the night-wisdom, and the protective wild without reducing it to “man’s best friend” alone.

In a Fire Horse year that loves speed and spectacle, Wolves refuse to abandon the pack mid-race. They may not be first to proclaim the dream — but they are often last to let it die. Guarded can look like cold from the outside. Underneath: devotion as enduring as winter. Nourish the flame without scorching the earth. That’s Mage work.

Find your pack. Keep the vow. Howl when it’s time — and hunt quietly when it’s wiser. 🐺🌙
